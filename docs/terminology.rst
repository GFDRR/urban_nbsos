Summary of NBSOS terminology
============================

The NBSOS uses a distinct set of terminology to describe various aspects of hazard exposure and nature-based solutions (NBS) benefits, as outlined below.

Exposure
--------
The extent of population exposed to hazards.

Priority areas
--------------
Indicate areas where NBS interventions are assumed to have the highest impact to reduce exposure to hazards. 

NBS Protection benefits
-----------------------
Identify the benefits provided by existing green spaces in terms of ecosystem service provision that are critical to maintain urban resilience by mitigating hazard exposure, supporting health and recreation, and enhancing biodiversity.

NBS Creation benefits
---------------------
Indicate the potential benefits derived from the implementation of NBS, including reducing hazard exposure, supporting health and recreation through accessible green areas, and enhancing biodiversity through improved ecological connectivity.  

NBS family
----------
Refers to a distinct group or category of nature-based solutions.
