The Urban Nature-Based Solutions Opportunity Scan
=================================================


The Nature-Based Solutions Opportunity Scan (NBSOS) is a to help identify and assess potential
impacts and benefits of nature-based solutions (NBS) in a specific area to help identify potentials for investment
opportunities for NbS implementation. In a wider sense, the NBSOS is a participatory process that facilitates this assessment. In a closer sense,
the NBSOS is a Python package to assess the potential impacts and benefits of nature-based solutions (NbS) in a given area of interest (AOI).

This manual describes the conceptual and technical background of the NBSOS, and provides guidance on the tool implementation and
step-by-step instructions on how to use the tool to conduct an NBS opportunity scan.
