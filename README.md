The Nature-based Solutions Opportunity Scan (NBSOS), a geospatial modeling tool and participatory process designed to overcome barriers to NBS adoption and accelerate the integration of NBS into early-stage urban resilience investment planning. The NBSOS combines globally available datasets with an analytical workflow to identify and prioritize multifunctional NBS investment opportunities aligned with local resilience goals.
