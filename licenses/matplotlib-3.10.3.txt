their canonical form.  They have been converted from OTF to TTF format
using Fontforge and this script:

  #!/usr/bin/env fontforge
  i=1
  while ( i<$argc )
    Open($argv[i])
    Generate($argv[i]:r + ".ttf")
    i = i+1
  endloop
